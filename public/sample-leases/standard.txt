Fictional sample lease excerpt for product demonstration only. This is not legal advice and is not a real lease form.

1. Parties and Property
This residential lease excerpt is between Harbor & Elm Homes, LLC, called Landlord in this sample, and Jordan Reed, called Tenant in this sample. The dwelling is the apartment at 214 Willow Lane, Unit 3B, Brookfield Heights, Fictional State 00000. The apartment includes ordinary use of one bedroom, one bathroom, the kitchen appliances already installed in the unit, and shared access to the laundry room. No garage, storage room, roof deck, or separate parking space is included unless listed in writing.

2. Lease Term
The initial lease term begins on August 1, 2026 and ends on July 31, 2027. Tenant may occupy the unit only as a private residence. Tenant should keep a copy of this lease and any written addenda because verbal promises are not part of this sample excerpt unless confirmed in writing by Landlord.

3. Monthly Rent
Rent is $1,425 per month, due on the 1st day of each month. Rent must be paid through the resident portal or another written payment method approved by Landlord. If the payment portal is unavailable, Tenant must promptly contact Landlord for written payment instructions. Partial payment does not waive any unpaid balance.

4. Security Deposit
Security deposit: $1,425 due at lease signing. The deposit may be used for unpaid rent, cleaning beyond ordinary use, missing keys, damage beyond ordinary wear, or other amounts Tenant owes under this lease excerpt. Tenant should ask for an itemized explanation of any deductions when the tenancy ends.

5. Late Fees and Returned Payments
Late fees: $75 if rent is not received by the 5th calendar day after it is due. If rent remains unpaid after the 8th calendar day, an additional daily charge of $15 may be added until the overdue rent is paid. Returned payment fee of $45 applies for each payment returned, rejected, or reversed by a bank or payment processor. Administrative fees may apply for notices, account research, or repeated payment reversals at the landlord's discretion.

6. Utility Accounts and Services
Utilities: Electricity, gas, internet, cable, and any separately billed trash service are paid by the resident. Landlord is responsible for water and sewer unless a written utility addendum says otherwise. Tenant must keep utility accounts active during the lease term and must not allow service interruptions caused by nonpayment. If a shared meter or estimated bill is used, Tenant may ask Landlord how the charge is calculated.

7. Maintenance and Repairs
Maintenance: Tenant must keep the unit clean, replace ordinary light bulbs, replace smoke detector batteries when needed, and report leaks, pests, broken locks, or appliance problems promptly. Tenant is responsible for minor repairs up to $100 per incident when the issue is caused by Tenant, a guest, a pet, or failure to report a problem promptly. Landlord is responsible for major structural repairs and repairs to building systems not caused by Tenant or Tenant's guests.

8. Landlord Entry
Landlord entry: Landlord may enter the unit with reasonable notice, generally at least 24 hours, for repairs, inspections, pest treatment, showing the unit to future renters or buyers, or checking a reported safety concern. Landlord may enter without prior notice in an emergency such as active water leakage, fire, suspected gas leak, or a condition that may cause immediate property damage.

9. Renewal and Notice
Automatic renewal: Unless either party gives at least 60 days' written notice before the end of the initial term, this lease automatically renews on a month-to-month basis. After renewal, rent may be adjusted with written notice. Notice period: Tenant must provide 60 days' written notice before moving out or choosing not to renew. Text messages, hallway conversations, and verbal statements are not enough unless Landlord confirms in writing that notice was received.

10. Pets, Guests, and Subletting
Pets are allowed only with prior written approval. Pet fee: $35 per month per approved pet. Unauthorized pets may result in cleaning charges, repair charges, or removal of approval for the pet. Guests may stay no more than 7 consecutive nights and no more than 14 total nights in any 60-day period without written approval. Subletting, short-term rental, room rental, or assignment of this lease is not allowed without Landlord's prior written consent.

11. Early Move-Out or Termination
Early termination fee of $1,200 may be charged if Tenant moves out before the lease term ends without a written agreement. Tenant may also remain responsible for rent until the unit is re-rented, subject to Landlord's ordinary efforts to find a replacement renter. Tenant should request any early move-out agreement in writing before returning keys.

12. Questions and Unclear Clauses
Some other administrative fees may apply for services or lease violations not listed above, including document replacement, lock changes, package handling, or account review. Amounts for these items may be updated by Landlord from time to time. Tenant should ask Landlord to identify every required monthly charge, every one-time move-in charge, and every fee that could be charged at move-out before signing.
